# CarePaw — The Story, In Simple Words

> This is the whole system explained as one story. No tech words. Just what happens.

## Meet Lina and Bingo

Lina is a pet owner. Bingo is her dog.

The clinic is run by one person — the Owner/Admin. Think of them as the manager + doctor in one.

---

## 1. Lina joins — like getting a house key

Lina makes an account with her email and password. Then she logs in.

It is like getting a house key. After that, she can go in and out freely.

---

## 2. Lina adds Bingo — like making an ID card

Lina adds her dog Bingo. She writes his name, what animal he is, his breed, if male or female, his birthday, and adds a photo.

It is like making an ID card for a family member.

---

## 3. Lina books a visit — like reserving a table

Lina picks Bingo, picks what she needs like checkup or vaccine, picks a day and time, and writes why, like "yearly shot" or "scratching his ear."

It is like reserving a table at a restaurant. The booking waits for the clinic to say yes.

---

## 4. The clinic answers — like a text back

The clinic sees Lina's booking on their list. They approve it. If the time does not work, they move it to a new time.

Lina gets a message on her phone. "Your visit is confirmed."

It is like the restaurant texting back, "Table ready for Saturday 10am."

---

## 5. Visit day — Lina checks in

Lina arrives at the clinic. The clinic checks her in. Bingo joins the line for that day.

People with no booking can still walk in. The clinic adds them to the line too.

---

## 6. Lina waits — like a Jollibee number

On her phone, Lina sees where Bingo is in line. "3rd in line... 2nd in line... You're next."

It is like taking a number at Jollibee. She does not need to ask the counter. The screen moves by itself.

---

## 7. Bingo's turn — the doctor calls

When it is Bingo's turn, the clinic calls them to the exam room. The line moves up for everyone.

If a visit is called off, it is marked cancelled.

---

## 8. Checkup — the doctor looks and writes

The clinic opens Bingo's file. They see all his past visits.

After the checkup, they write new visit notes. What they found. What they did. What Lina should watch for at home.

Old notes are never erased. It is like a report card written in pen. If there is a mistake, they add a new note on top. They never erase the old one.

---

## 9. Vaccine and medicine — if Bingo needs it

If Bingo needs a vaccine or deworming, they write it down in his history. Name, date given, next due date.

If Bingo needs medicine, they write the instructions. What medicine, how much, how often, for how many days.

Then they prepare the medicine and update the stock count, like "20 bottles left."

---

## 10. Medicine shelf — like a sari-sari store

The clinic keeps a list of all medicines. They know how many are left. They know which ones expire first. They know what came in and what went out.

It is like a sari-sari store shelf with a notebook for every can in and out.

If stock is low, it is flagged.

---

## 11. Camera scan — like a photocopy that needs a check

The clinic can point the phone camera at a medicine box or receipt. The phone reads the words.

But the phone is never trusted alone. A person always checks and presses confirm before saving.

It is like a student photocopying notes. The teacher must check and sign first.

---

## 12. Lina goes home — messages and history

Lina gets a message. Visit done. Medicine ready. Or a reminder for the next visit.

Anytime later, Lina can open Bingo's health history and see every visit, vaccine, and deworming.

It is like getting an SMS, plus a folder she can open anytime.

---

## The whole story in one line

```
Join → Add pet → Book → Get confirmed → Check in → Wait in line → See doctor → Get notes and medicine → Get message → View history anytime
```

---

## Safety rules, in simple words

1. Do not tape your key to the door. Secrets stay secret.
2. Teacher must sign the photocopy. Scans need a person to confirm.
3. Report card in pen. Old visit notes are never deleted.
4. Only key-holders enter locked rooms. Every sensitive action checks permission.
5. All or nothing. Related saves happen together.
6. Check the form first. All inputs are checked.
7. CCTV is on. Sensitive actions are logged — who did what and when.
